# Per-Product Control

Every product has its own **Social Media Settings** box on the product edit screen. Use it to write special social wording for a product or to keep a product out of social output.

## The box

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| Enable social sharing output for this product | On by default. Untick it to remove the social preview tags and the share buttons for this product. Search structured data (what Google reads for product results) is not affected. |
| Social Media Title | Your own title for social posts. Up to 60 characters, with a live counter. Leave empty to use the product name. |
| Social Media Description | Your own description for social posts. Up to 155 characters, with a live counter. Leave empty to use the product description. |
| Social Media Image | Information only. Shows "Featured image will be used" or "No featured image set". |

The empty title and description fields show a grey hint of exactly what will be published when you leave them empty.

When you untick the switch, the title and description fields hide. Your text is kept, so you can switch output back on later without retyping.

## What "off" removes and what it keeps

Removed for that product:

- The social preview tags (Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest).
- The share buttons, including ones added by the shortcode.
- The share script and styles.

Kept:

- The structured data Google uses for search results.
- The product in your sitemap.
- Category and shop pages that list the product.

## The Social column

On **Products > All Products** the plugin adds a **Social** column next to the product name:

| Shown | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Auto | Everything is generated automatically. |
| Custom | The product has its own social title or description. |
| Off | Social output is switched off for the product. |

The words are shown, not only colours, so the column is readable without colour.

## Overview counts

The **Overview** tab counts published products with custom social text and with social output off, so you can see how many products you have changed.
