# Add Your Store Logo and Social Profiles for Google

Goal: tell Google your store logo and which social pages belong to your store, so it can connect them to your search listing.

## Steps

1. Go to **WB Plugins > Open Graph** and click the **Structured Data** tab.
2. Under **Product Schema**, switch **Structured Data** on. The logo and profiles are published only while this switch is on. See [Structured Data](../features/structured-data.md).
3. Under **Organization**, make sure **Organization Schema** is on.
4. Click **Choose Image** next to **Organization Logo** and pick your logo. If you leave it empty, the plugin uses your theme logo, then your Site Icon.
5. In **Social Profiles**, type one address per line, for example:

```
https://www.facebook.com/yourstore
https://www.instagram.com/yourstore
```

6. Click **Save Settings**.

Only complete web addresses starting with http:// or https:// are kept. Anything else is dropped when you save.

## How to check it worked

1. Go to **WB Plugins > Open Graph > Overview** and click **Google Rich Results**.
2. Paste your shop page address and run the test.
3. Look for the Organization item with your logo and profile links.

## If it doesn't work

See [Structured data and sitemap problems](../troubleshooting/structured-data-and-sitemap-problems.md).
