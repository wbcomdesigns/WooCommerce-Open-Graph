# Add Share Buttons and Choose Their Style and Position

Goal: show share buttons on every product page, in the style and place you like.

## Steps

1. Go to **WB Plugins > Open Graph** and click the **Share Buttons** tab.
2. Make sure **Enable Share Buttons** is on.
3. Switch **Email Button** on if you want shoppers to be able to email the product. It is off by default.
4. Choose a **Button Style**:
   - **Modern**: clean buttons with smooth hover animation.
   - **Classic**: traditional rectangular buttons.
   - **Minimal**: icons only, no text.
5. Choose a **Button Position**:
   - **After Add to Cart Button**
   - **Before Add to Cart Button**
   - **After Product Summary**
   - **After Product Tabs**
6. Click **Save Settings**.
7. Click the **General** tab. Each network button shows only if its switch is on under **Social Platforms** (Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, Pinterest, WhatsApp). Switch off the ones you do not want and click **Save Settings**.

The **Copy** button is always shown.

## How to check it worked

Open a product page in your store. Under "Share this product:" you see one button per network you left on, plus Copy. Click Copy and the button briefly says "Link copied!".
