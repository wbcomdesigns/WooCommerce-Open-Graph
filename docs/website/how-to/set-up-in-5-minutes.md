# Set Up in 5 Minutes

Goal: get your products looking good when shared, with the fewest clicks.

## What is already on after you activate

| Feature | Default |
|---|---|
| Social preview tags for Facebook, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, Pinterest and WhatsApp | On |
| Share buttons on product pages (Modern style, after the Add to Cart button) | On |
| Email share button | Off |
| Product sitemap (500 products per file) | On |
| Structured Data (extra Google data) | Off |
| Override SEO Titles | Off |
| Delete Data On Uninstall | Off |

## Steps

1. Go to **WB Plugins > Open Graph**. You land on the **Overview** tab.
2. Read the **Current Configuration** list. Any row that says **Off** has a **Turn on** link next to it.
3. Click the **General** tab.
4. Under **Platform Accounts**, enter your **Twitter Username** (letters, numbers and underscores only, with or without the @). Add a **Facebook App ID** only if you have one.
5. Under **Image & Content**, click **Choose Image** next to **Default Social Image** and pick a store image. Products without a photo will use it. A wide image about 1200 by 630 pixels works well.
6. Click **Save Settings**.
7. Click the **Share Buttons** tab. Choose a **Button Style** and **Button Position**, switch on **Email Button** if you want it, then click **Save Settings**.
8. Click the **Structured Data** tab. If you want Google to know your logo and social profiles, switch on **Structured Data**, set your **Organization Logo** and **Social Profiles**, then click **Save Settings**. See [Add Your Store Logo and Social Profiles for Google](add-logo-and-social-profiles-for-google.md).
9. Open one product on your store and look for the "Share this product:" buttons.

## How to check it worked

1. On the **Overview** tab, click **Facebook Debugger**.
2. Paste the link to one of your products and run it.
3. You should see your product photo, name, description and price.

More detail: [Check How a Product Looks When Shared](check-how-a-product-looks-when-shared.md).
