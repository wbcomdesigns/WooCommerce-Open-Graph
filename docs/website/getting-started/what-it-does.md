# What Open Graph for WooCommerce Does

When someone shares a link to your product on Facebook, X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp or Pinterest, the network opens your page and reads a few hidden tags to decide what the shared post looks like. This plugin writes those tags for you.

The result: a shared product link shows the product photo, the product name, a short description and the price, instead of a bare link or a wrong image.

## What you get

- **Rich previews.** Product, category, tag and shop pages carry the tags that social networks read.
- **Share buttons.** Shoppers get buttons on the product page for Facebook, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, Pinterest, WhatsApp, Email and Copy Link.
- **Control per product.** You can write a different social title and description for any product, or switch social output off for one product.
- **Extra search data.** Optional structured data that helps Google understand your store (logo, social profiles, breadcrumbs, product brand and codes).
- **Product sitemap.** An XML sitemap of your products and categories for search engines.

## What it does not do

- It does not post to social networks for you.
- It does not replace your SEO plugin. It works next to Yoast, Rank Math or SEOPress and skips tags they already print. See [Use with an SEO Plugin](../how-to/use-with-seo-plugins.md).
- It does not change how your product page looks to shoppers, apart from the share buttons.

## Where the settings are

Everything is at **WB Plugins > Open Graph** in your WordPress admin menu. There is also a **Settings** link under the plugin name on the Plugins screen.
