# Open Graph for WooCommerce

Open Graph for WooCommerce makes your store's products show rich previews when
their links are shared on social platforms, and helps search engines understand
each product page.

When someone pastes a product URL into Facebook, X (Twitter), LinkedIn,
Pinterest, or WhatsApp, this plugin has already placed the meta tags those
platforms read, so the link unfurls with the product image, title,
description, and price instead of a bare URL.

## What the plugin does

- Adds Open Graph and Twitter Card meta tags to product, product category,
  product tag, and shop pages.
- Adds Schema.org (JSON-LD) structured data for products, breadcrumbs, the
  store organization, categories, and the shop page.
- Adds social share buttons to single product pages, with Facebook, X
  (Twitter), LinkedIn, Pinterest, WhatsApp, email, and copy-link actions.
- Generates a dedicated XML sitemap for products, categories, and brands, and
  advertises it in `robots.txt`.
- Lets you override the social title and description per product, or turn the
  plugin's output off for a single product.

## Who it is for

Store owners who want product links to look good when shared, and who want
product-specific structured data (price, availability, brand, ratings, GTIN,
MPN) that general SEO plugins often leave out.
